# Pranish (Nish) Pakwan

I'm Nish. I build AI tools, and right now I'm working on HiredStack, a product to help people manage their job search. I also built Marketing Studio to turn my research and notes into drafts I can review for HiredStack.

My background is in analytics and business systems, with experience in e-commerce, car rental, sales, and marketing. I like working on questions where the answer will shape a decision. At Vanderbilt, the question was which online graduate programs to explore next.

## Selected work

### [Marketing Studio](https://github.com/nishpn01/marketing-studio)

I wanted a way to turn HiredStack's research and notes into marketing drafts I could review. I built a workflow where one agent writes the draft and separate reviewers check the claims and the writing. I get the draft, its sources, and both reviews together so I can decide what to use.

### [Inbox Agent](https://github.com/nishpn01/inbox-agent)

I built Inbox Agent to sort email with labels I can adjust. It explains each suggestion and uses my corrections on the next run. You can see how that works in the [demo](https://inbox-agent-tan.vercel.app), which uses 49 fictional emails.

### [AI Document Intelligence](https://github.com/nishpn01/ai-document-intelligence)

During my Pfizer externship, I looked at how reliably an AI system could answer questions about PDFs. I compared ways to extract text and retrieve relevant passages, then checked five answers against the source documents to see where the system went wrong.

### [Program Viability Assessment](https://github.com/nishpn01/program-viability-assessment)

To find online graduate programs worth exploring for Vanderbilt, I joined federal education and labor data in PostgreSQL and scored 1,254 candidates. That gave me five programs to recommend for a closer look. The [Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/pranish.p7454/viz/OnlineProgramViabilityAssessment/Program_Overview_Dashboard2) shows the evidence behind the shortlist.
